LeetCode 3913 - Sort Vowels by Frequency

Approach:

Count frequencies of all vowels ('a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u') using a HashMap and keep unique vowels in a LinkedHashSet.
Sort vowels in descending order of their frequency:
- Repeatedly select the vowel with maximum frequency from the set, append all its occurrences to a list lst, and remove it from the set.
Iterate through the characters of s:
- Whenever a vowel is encountered, replace it with the next vowel from lst in frequency order.
- Consonants remain in their original positions.
Return the reconstructed string.

Complexity:

Time: O(n) where n is s.length(), scanning vowels in linear time and sorting at most 5 vowel types in O(1).
Space: O(n) auxiliary space to store the vowel list and output character array.

Key Takeaway:
Isolating and sorting only the target vowel subset by frequency allows in-place replacement while preserving consonant indices.
