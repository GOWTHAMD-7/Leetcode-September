LeetCode 648 - Replace Words

Approach:

Split the input sentence sen into an array of words arr using space as a delimiter.
For each dictionary root s:
- Compare prefix of length s.length() against each word in arr.
- If the prefix matches, replace the word in arr with root s.
Recombine all modified words using StringBuilder with space separators and return the resulting sentence.

Complexity:

Time: O(d * w * L) where d is dict.size(), w is the word count in sen, and L is the length of dictionary root string.
Space: O(w * m) auxiliary space to store split words and intermediate string representations.

Key Takeaway:
Iterating through dictionary roots and matching prefixes against sentence words replaces derivatives with corresponding roots in-place.
