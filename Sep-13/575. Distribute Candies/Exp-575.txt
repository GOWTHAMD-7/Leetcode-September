LeetCode 575 - Distribute Candies

Approach:

Insert all candy types into a HashSet to count the total number of unique candy types (s).
The maximum number of unique candies Alice can receive is constrained by:
- The total distinct types available (s).
- The maximum candy allowance she can eat (candy.length / 2).
Return Math.min(s, candy.length / 2).

Complexity:

Time: O(n) where n is candy.length, scanning the array once to insert into the HashSet.
Space: O(n) auxiliary space to store unique candy types in the set.

Key Takeaway:
Greedy selection of one of each candy type up to the eating quota (n / 2) maximizes candy variety.
