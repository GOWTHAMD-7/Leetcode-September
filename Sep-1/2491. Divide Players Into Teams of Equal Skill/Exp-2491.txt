LeetCode 2491 - Divide Players Into Teams of Equal Skill

Approach:

Sort the array.
Pair the smallest-skilled player with the largest-skilled player.
The required team skill is arr[0] + arr[n-1].
Use two pointers, one from the beginning and one from the end.
If every pair has the same total skill, add the product of the two players' skills.
If any pair has a different total skill, return -1.

Complexity:

Time: O(n log n) - sorting
Space: O(1) auxiliary space

Key Takeaway:
Sorting + two pointers allows us to efficiently form pairs with equal total skill.
