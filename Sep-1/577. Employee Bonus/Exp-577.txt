LeetCode 577 - Employee Bonus

Approach:

Use a LEFT JOIN to include all employees, even those who don't have a bonus record.
Select the employee name and bonus.
Filter employees whose bonus is less than 1000.
Include employees with no bonus using b.bonus IS NULL.

Complexity:

Time: O(n) average, depending on database indexing/join implementation.
Space: O(n) depending on the execution plan.

Key Takeaway:
Use LEFT JOIN when you need to keep all records from the left table, and handle missing values using IS NULL.
