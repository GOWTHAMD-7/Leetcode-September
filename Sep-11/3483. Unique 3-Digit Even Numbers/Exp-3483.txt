LeetCode 3483 - Unique 3-Digit Even Numbers

Approach:

Count frequencies of all digits [0-9] in an array arr of size 10.
Use a 3-stage backtracking search (ones -> twos -> threes) to form unique 3-digit numbers:
- Early pruning: if no even digits (0, 2, 4, 6, 8) remain in arr, immediately return since an even number cannot be terminated.
- Hundreds position (ones): pick a digit from 1 to 9 (avoiding leading zero).
- Tens position (twos): pick a digit from 0 to 9.
- Units position (threes): pick an available even digit from {0, 2, 4, 6, 8} and increment ret.
Restore digit counts during backtracking to allow other branches to explore.

Complexity:

Time: O(n + 9 * 10 * 5) = O(n) where n is dig.length, bounding backtracking exploration to at most 450 constant-time checks.
Space: O(1) auxiliary space using a fixed size-10 frequency array.

Key Takeaway:
Iterating over digit values (0-9) rather than array indices guarantees unique number generation without requiring set-based deduplication.
