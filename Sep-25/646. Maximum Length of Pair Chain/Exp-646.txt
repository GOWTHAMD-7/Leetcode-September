LeetCode 646 - Maximum Length of Pair Chain

Approach:

Apply greedy interval scheduling:
- Sort the pair intervals in ascending order by their end times: a[1] - b[1].
- Maintain max (the right endpoint of the last chosen pair in the chain) and cnt (chain length).
- Iterate through the sorted pairs:
  - If the start of the current pair arr[i][0] is strictly greater than max, extend the chain: increment cnt and update max = arr[i][1].
Return cnt as the maximum length of the pair chain.

Complexity:

Time: O(n log n) where n is arr.length, dominated by the sorting step.
Space: O(1) auxiliary space beyond the sorting space.

Key Takeaway:
Sorting intervals by end time and greedily choosing non-overlapping pairs maximizes the total number of chained intervals.
