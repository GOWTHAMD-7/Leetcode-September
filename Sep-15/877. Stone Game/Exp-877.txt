LeetCode 877 - Stone Game

Approach:

Analyze parity of pile positions and game constraints:
- The total number of piles is even (len % 2 == 0), and total sum of stones is odd (no ties possible).
- The piles can be partitioned into two sets: even-indexed piles and odd-indexed piles.
- The first player (Alex) can choose to take either all even-indexed piles or all odd-indexed piles:
  - If Alex picks the first pile (even index), Bob must choose between two odd-indexed piles.
  - On the next turn, Alex can pick another even-indexed pile, maintaining control over the parity choice throughout the entire game.
- Because the total stone count is odd, the sum of even piles cannot equal the sum of odd piles.
- Alex simply chooses the set with the strictly larger sum, guaranteeing victory every time.
Return true.

Complexity:

Time: O(1) mathematical deduction.
Space: O(1) auxiliary space.

Key Takeaway:
With an even number of piles and an odd total sum, the first player can always force taking all even or all odd positions to guarantee a win.
