LeetCode 417 - Pacific Atlantic Water Flow

Approach:

Invert the flow direction: start DFS from the ocean boundaries and search uphill to cells of equal or higher elevation.
Use a status grid pac to record reachability:
- Pass 1 (Pacific Ocean): Initiate DFS from top row and left column, marking reachable cells with 1.
- Pass 2 (Atlantic Ocean): Initiate DFS from bottom row and right column, marking newly reachable cells with 2 and cells previously reachable by Pacific with 3.
Iterate through the grid and collect all coordinates [i, j] where pac[i][j] == 3 (reachable from both oceans).

Complexity:

Time: O(m * n) where m and n are matrix dimensions, since each cell is visited a constant number of times across both DFS passes.
Space: O(m * n) auxiliary space for the status matrix and recursion call stack.

Key Takeaway:
Searching backwards (uphill) from boundary ocean cells avoids redundant paths and reduces an O((m*n)^2) search to linear time in grid size.
